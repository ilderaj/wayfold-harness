# Provenance and distribution

This is an unreleased WayFold Harness candidate. Repository-owned additions have no new public licensing grant in this migration. Release licensing remains a separate gate.

The planning-with-files lifecycle helpers derive from the SWF canonical overlay at baseline commit 8418d2a2f3663a874773beb58df002873723866f, with candidate path, binding and local sync changes. The upstream planning-with-files MIT notice is preserved beside the skill. No Matt, Cloudflare, Kami, Figma, Pen, Claima or WeRead implementation or private asset is bundled.

Retained legacy .harness reads preserve existing bindings and archive evidence only; they do not execute an old runtime.
